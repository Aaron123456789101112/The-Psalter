Listen offline

Save recordings to this phone and they play with no internet: on a plane, in the country, anywhere without signal.

Put the Psalter on your home screen

It will open by itself, full screen, like any other app, instead of in the web browser.

It will then open by itself, full screen, like any other app, instead of in Safari.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the Psalter from its new icon, and save your recordings there. On an iPhone, the home-screen Psalter keeps its own saved recordings, separate from Safari.

    Saved recordings stay on this phone until you remove them. New psalms and chapters aren’t saved by themselves: open this page and press Save again to add them.

    Make a clip

    Choose a reading and the verses you want. Your phone turns them into a short video with the words on screen, ready for Facebook.

    On an iPhone, Save video usually opens the Share menu: choose Save Video (it goes to Photos) or Facebook. If it saves to Downloads instead, open the Files app, tap Downloads, tap the video, tap the Share button, and choose Save Video or Facebook. Then in Facebook, start a post (or a Reel for a tall video), tap Photo/Video, and pick it.
